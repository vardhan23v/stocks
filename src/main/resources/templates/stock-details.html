    Technical indicators

    SMA 20
    Average close of the last 20 sessions. The price is from it.
    SMA 50
    Longer-term trend. SMA 20 above SMA 50 is commonly read as a short-term uptrend.
    EMA 20
    Like SMA 20 but weights recent closes more, so it reacts faster.
    RSI 14
    MACD
    EMA 12 minus EMA 26: positive when short-term momentum is stronger.
    Signal line
    9-period EMA of MACD. MACD is it (histogram ).
    20-session change
    Price momentum over roughly one month of trading.
    Volume vs average
    Latest volume divided by the 20-session average.

    These readings are common analytical interpretations, not guaranteed buy or sell signals.
