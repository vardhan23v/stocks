    How the score works

    Each enabled rule looks at one indicator and gives a signal of +1 (bullish), -1 (bearish) or 0 (neutral). The signal is multiplied by the rule's weight, and the score is the sum. A stock needs at least 60 stored daily bars; with fewer the engine shows "Insufficient data" instead of guessing.
